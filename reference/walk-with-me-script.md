Walk With Me — 1-Minute Listening Script
An original law-firm dialogue for practicing fast speech and everyday native expressions. About 175 words, roughly 1 minute at natural fast speed.
Scene
Setting: a busy law firm, 8:55 a.m. Two lawyers talk while walking quickly down a hallway.
Characters:
• Ryan Cole: senior partner, confident, always in a hurry (American accent)
• Maya Lin: junior associate, quick and sharp (American accent)
Korean scene summary (for Levels 1–2): 고객이 거래를 포기하려 하자, 신입 변호사 마야가 바쁜 상사 라이언에게 해결책을 보고하는 장면이에요.

VIDEO OVERVIEW
A 60-second original live-action scene for an English listening app. Purpose: train Korean learners to understand FAST natural American speech and common conversational expressions. Two lawyers have a quick, witty "walk-and-talk" conversation down a modern law-firm hallway. The dialogue must be spoken EXACTLY as written, at fast natural native speed, with clear audio. Original characters; they must not resemble any real actor or TV show character.

FOCUS POINT
- Fast speech: about 170–180 words per minute, almost no pauses between lines, speakers sometimes start talking right as the other finishes.
- Natural connected speech: say "wanna" and "gonna" as written; let "got a sec", "look at you", "what're you gonna" blend together naturally.
- Everyday native expressions: cold feet, cut to the chase, on the fence, play hardball, drop the ball, a long shot.

SETTING
- A high-end corporate law firm in Manhattan, New York, 8:55 a.m. on a weekday.
- Long hallway with glass-walled offices on one side and floor-to-ceiling windows on the other, showing the city skyline in soft morning light.
- Polished light-gray stone floor, white walls, warm wood accents, modern lighting.
- A few blurred background employees walking past with folders and coffee (out of focus, silent).
- The hallway ends at a glass conference-room door.

STYLE
- Realistic, cinematic live action, modern legal drama look, 4K, 16:9.
- Smooth steadicam tracking shot moving backward in front of the two characters as they walk toward the camera; occasional over-the-shoulder angle.
- Shallow depth of field: faces sharp, background softly blurred.
- Color: cool blue-gray office tones with warm golden sunlight from the windows.
- Natural, realistic lip-sync for every line.

MOOD
- Fast-paced, confident, focused and slightly tense, with quick witty energy.
- Professional but friendly: they respect each other.
- The final two lines turn playful: Maya teases Ryan, and he smiles.

ON-SCREEN ENGLISH TEXT (only these; no subtitles, no other text)
- 0–3 s: small white title in the lower-left corner, clean sans-serif font: "Walk With Me"
- 0–3 s: small caption under it: "8:55 AM"
- Spoken dialogue must NEVER appear as text on screen.

CHARACTERS (keep appearance IDENTICAL in every shot: same face, hair, body, clothes, accessories and props; do not change anything)

Wears a fitted charcoal-gray two-piece suit, crisp white shirt, solid navy-blue tie, silver wristwatch on his left wrist, black leather shoes. Carries nothing. Confident, fast walker, slight smirk. Wears a navy-blue tailored blazer over a white silk blouse, slim black trousers, low black heels, small gold stud earrings. Holds a black tablet in her left hand. Sharp, energetic, quick-thinking. SHOT 1 (0–15 s)
Maya hurries out of a glass office and catches up with Ryan, who is already walking fast down the hallway. They walk side by side toward the camera.
MAYA: "Ryan, got a sec? We've got a problem."
RYAN (not slowing down): "Walk with me. I'm already late. What's going on?"
MAYA: "Henderson's getting cold feet. He wants to pull out of the deal."
RYAN (stops for half a second, then keeps walking): "You're kidding me. We're this close to closing."
MAYA: "I know. He says the numbers don't add up."

SHOT 2 (15–30 s)
Same hallway, same walk, same characters and clothes. Ryan looks at Maya while walking; Maya checks her tablet.
RYAN: "Then let's cut to the chase. Is he bluffing, or is he actually walking?"
MAYA: "Honestly? I think he's on the fence."
RYAN: "Good. On the fence I can work with. Set up a call for ten."
MAYA (small proud smile): "Already did. I figured you'd wanna jump on it."

SHOT 3 (30–45 s)
Same hallway, closer two-shot, still walking fast. Ryan looks impressed.
RYAN: "Look at you, one step ahead. What're you gonna tell him?"
MAYA: "That if he backs out now, he's leaving money on the table."
RYAN: "And if he doesn't buy it?"
MAYA (confident): "Then we play hardball. Their offer expires Friday."
RYAN: "That's a long shot, but it might work. Don't drop the ball on this one."

SHOT 4 (45–60 s)
They arrive at the glass conference-room door. Ryan stops with his hand on the door handle and turns to Maya.
MAYA: "I won't. I've got this."
RYAN: "I know you do. That's why it's yours. Ten o'clock. Don't be late."
MAYA (raising an eyebrow, teasing): "Says the guy who's already late."
RYAN (small smile, opens the door): "Touché."
Ryan walks into the conference room; Maya smiles and turns away. End.
